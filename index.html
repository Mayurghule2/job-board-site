Questions

Will this get me a job?

No kit can promise that. It helps you write clearer, more specific outreach messages and keep track of what you send. You still need to reach out consistently and have a resume you are proud of.

Is this for freshers or experienced professionals?

Both. Every message has a fresher and an experienced version, and there is a resume template for each.
